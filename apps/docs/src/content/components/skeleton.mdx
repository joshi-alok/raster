---
title: Skeleton
group: Display
order: 7
summary: Placeholder blocks in the shape of the content that is loading, with a slow pulse.
use:
  - Hold the shape of content while it loads.
avoid:
  - text: A task of known length.
    instead: progress-bar
  - text: An area with nothing to show.
    instead: empty-state
props:
  - name: shape
    type: '"text" | "heading" | "block"'
    default: '"text"'
    description: A line of body text, a heading, or a 16:9 block for media.
  - name: width
    type: string
    description: Any CSS length, such as "60%". Defaults to the full width.
a11y:
  - Skeletons are hidden from screen readers. Put aria-busy="true" on the area that is loading, with a role and a name (a group for a card, a region for a major part of the page), and remove aria-busy when the content arrives.
  - The pulse is a slow change of opacity, with no gradient; under reduced motion the blocks do not move.
  - In forced-colours mode each block is outlined.
---
